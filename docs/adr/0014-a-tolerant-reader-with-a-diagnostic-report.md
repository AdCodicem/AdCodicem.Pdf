# 14. A tolerant reader with a diagnostic report

Date: 2026-09-12

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Real PDFs are frequently non-conforming, and a strict reader fails where every reader on the
market succeeds. The report also lets a caller track the quality of incoming files.

## Decision

Cross-reference rebuilding, recovery from malformed
objects, and a structured report of anomalies and repairs.

## Consequences

Recorded so the choice is not silently revisited.
