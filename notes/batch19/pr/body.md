## What this changes

Batch 19 of M02's step 4: where the chain's sections lie, and what it reports of one it reads only in part.

- **A section the chain names is read where it is (#188, #281).** It is there where its offset holds the `xref` keyword or a stream whose dictionary gives `/Type /XRef`. One that is there and cannot be read is reported where it is, under `xref.section-unreadable`, and never looked for nearby. The search near an offset that holds no section passes over every place where a section the chain reached starts, told by where its first token starts or, for a stream reached at a sign of its header, by where its digits start (#327). It takes the nearest place that reads as a section, or that declares itself one and cannot be read, which is then the section found moved, reported where it lies. A place declares itself only where its keyword or header is a token its line reads, lexed alone from its start, read back up to 4,096 bytes; an object that gives only a `/W` stays a guess.
- **The search near an offset reaches 512 bytes either side of it, wherever it falls (#197).** A window that does not start the file reads one byte before its start, and a header or a section counts only where it starts inside it, so the window's edge no longer cuts a header's number.
- **A header with comments between its tokens reads as a load reads it (#261).** The search near a row, the rebuild's scan and the search near an offset lex a line that holds a `%` from its start with the load's lexer, in one linear walk that allocates nothing. The rule a damaged stream's `endstream` search stops at, and the parser's end of the containers an object left open, take such a header too, decided alike however a source parts its reads.
- **A cross-reference stream whose rows cannot be read hands its `/Prev` on (#208).** A width negative or no number, a fault of its numbering, a deferred `/Length` no `endstream` confirms, data its filters decode nothing of: the older sections are read and judged, and its dictionary is never taken for the trailer. Data left at a filter the library does not know, or Flate data corrupt before its first byte, holds no rows: the filter pipeline says so, and the encoded bytes are no longer read as rows.
- **A section read only in part is kept and reported once (#207, #210, #222).** A table the end of the file cuts after a row, a row that is no row ending a subsection, a stream that holds fewer rows than it declares: `xref.section-unreadable`, at the section, its message saying which rows stand. A table cut before its first row cannot be read. No older section's row stands for a number such a section lost, nor for one a stream that cannot be read declares, nor for what a classic table an `/XRefStm` names lost where what follows its fault cannot be known. The refused ranges are kept to 4,096, an internal constant (ADR 34), every number refused past them, and the rebuild finds those objects when they are asked for. A table that holds no subsection reads as indexing nothing, `xref.section-empty`, a repair. `trailer.root-recovered` is reported once a document.
- **Every `/XRefStm` naming is recorded (#156).** A stream two tables name is read once and counted for each. One that names no cross-reference stream of its revision is `xref.hybrid-stream-lost`, a warning, the index left incomplete, and is not followed where its table's `/Prev` names the same section: `xref.chain-cycle` and `xref.chain-loop` keep to `/Prev`. The search near a hybrid table's `/Prev` tells the table's own stream by where it lies and by its `/Prev`, trying 32 places a document at most. A stream it passes over that declares itself, which the chain never reads, is `xref.stream-passed-over`, a warning, withheld in a document where a limit may have hidden what the chain would read.
- **What a rows guard stopped is judged as a whole read judges it (#264).** The `/Prev` and `/XRefStm` of a section that `MaxXRefSectionLength`, `MaxDecodedStreamLength` or `MaxIndexEntries` stopped are judged, nothing past the guard read (ADR 34, amended on 2026-11-04). A section a link names is read from the first token past its white space, the white space and the dictionary each bounded by `MaxTrailerLength`, a classic table's white space counting toward `MaxXRefSectionLength` as well (#289); white space past the bound reaches `limit.trailer`.
- **A row of a reserved type reads as the null object (#221).** No older row stands for it, and the reader reports `xref.entry-reserved`, a warning, until a rebuild for another fault reads its object as the scan finds it.
- **The validator (#156, #221, #222).** Three new rules: `xref.subsection-missing` and `xref.entry-type-reserved`, warnings, and `xref.hybrid-stream-wrong`, an error. `file.size-wrong` counts a stream two tables name for each, judges its `/Size` where the oldest names it, and judges no chain that holds a section read in part or ends in a loop. `xref.section-malformed` covers a section read in part, `xref.offset-imprecise` counts each offset written once, and `file.trailer-value-wrong` judges a handed `/Prev`, and a hybrid stream's own `/Prev`, as written.
- **A count agrees with its noun, and a distance says "before" or "after" (#241).** Through `CountText`, which the reader and the validator share, each site keeping its digit grouping (#326). The diagnostics count apart the guards' reports their capacity drops, so that a reading rolled back beside a guard counts only those as left out.
- **#317's timing test is steadied.** Its probe takes a validation's default room for findings, 1,000, and times four probes back to back each turn under its bound of 8. Under four busy loops, 300 of 300 runs of the classes together and 100 of 100 alone passed, at ratios of 2.1 to 7.7, and the scan of each member's header the test guards against was caught in 45 of 45 runs, at 13.1 to 21.9. The timing of 1,024 chained streams that cannot be read is held under 12: 3.2 to 6.2 over 300 runs under the same load, up to 10.0 with builds beside them.
- **Benchmarks and test support.** `StreamEndSearchBenchmarks` and `ObjectScanBenchmarks` are new. `HybridFiles`, `NearbyFiles`, `PartialFiles`, `LostStreamFiles`, `ReservedRowFiles`, `StoppedSectionFiles` and `UndecodedStreamFiles` build the files, and `CappedReadSource`, `CutShortSource` and `ShortCountSource` serve short reads.
